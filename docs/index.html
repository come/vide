vide/

A minimal Android launcher designed to make you use your phone less. No icons. No colors. No animations. Just text.

Download APK Source code

Why

Every launcher is designed to keep you engaged — colorful icons, widgets, feeds, suggestions. vide/ does the opposite. It strips your home screen down to plain text, removes visual stimulation, and adds just enough friction to make you think twice before opening an app.

Features

Text-only home

Most-used apps as plain text, auto-sorted by usage frequency.

Smart search

Find apps and contacts with fuzzy matching. Call, SMS, WhatsApp, email in one tap.

Calendar glance

Upcoming events next to the clock. Scrollable. Tap to open.

Notification center

Mark important. Swipe to dismiss. Filter by category. Clear all skips what matters.

Design principles

Monochrome only No visual reward for unlocking
No app icons Removes brand recognition dopamine
No animations Nothing to watch, nothing to wait for
Single font Uniform, calm, utilitarian
Friction by design Search to find, text to read, no shortcuts
